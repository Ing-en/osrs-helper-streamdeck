# OSRS Helper: Marketplace listing

## Name
OSRS Helper

## Description
Old School RuneScape on your Stream Deck. The Idle Alert key flashes when RuneLite's Idle Notifier says your character has gone idle, and clears as soon as you're back in the game. Time Tracking keys count down your herb, tree, fruit tree and other farming patches, the farming contract, bird houses, timers and stopwatches, then flash green when they're ready. Give each key an account or let it watch them all, and press it to bring that game client to the front.

Requirements: Windows and the RuneLite client for Old School RuneScape. No extra plugins are needed: for idle alerts, set RuneLite's notification "Request focus" option to Taskbar; countdowns are read from RuneLite's Time Tracking, which saves every few minutes. Everything stays on your computer and nothing is sent over the internet.

## Release notes (1.0.0)
First release: Idle Alert and Time Tracking keys, per-account watching, and press to bring RuneLite to the front.
