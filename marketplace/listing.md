# OSRS Helper: Marketplace listing

## Name
OSRS Helper

## Description
Old School RuneScape on your Stream Deck. The Idle Alert key flashes the moment your character goes idle, stops moving, leaves combat or is about to be logged out, and clears as soon as you're playing again. Time Tracking keys count down your herb, tree, fruit tree and other farming patches, the farming contract, bird houses, timers and stopwatches, then flash green when they're ready. Give each key an account or let it watch them all, and press it to bring that game client to the front.

Requirements: the RuneLite client for Old School RuneScape, with the free "Stream Deck" plugin from RuneLite's Plugin Hub installed. Idle alerts use RuneLite's Idle Notifier; countdowns use RuneLite's Time Tracking. Everything stays on your computer: RuneLite writes a small status file that this plugin reads, and nothing is sent over the internet.

## Release notes (1.0.0)
First release: Idle Alert and Time Tracking keys, per-account watching, and press to bring RuneLite to the front.
